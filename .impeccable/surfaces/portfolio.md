# Portfolio

Mode: **Experience**

## Purpose and audience

Personal professional portfolio for Urias Machado Santos (Sairu), a software developer in Feira de Santana, Brazil. Recruiters should be able to assess actual software work, inspect evidence and explore AI Builder potential without confusing prototypes with production experience.

## Intended experience

Lead with a dominant original SAIRU wordmark in industrial yellow on warm black. Move through a typography-led editorial sequence: identity and invitation, three real demo previews, biography and paid history, technology evidence, local terminal and direct contact.

Primary actions: open a demo, change its input, inspect the result, read source/tests and get in touch. The three project rows are the central product evidence. Screenshots must show the real browser demos.

## Composition and interaction

- Hero: original angular SVG wordmark, technical margin, yellow type strip, asymmetric copy and action.
- Projects: continuous numbered rows with screenshots, honest descriptions, test-demo buttons and source links.
- Biography: yellow section with factual roles and studies.
- Toolkit: keyboard-operable selection reveals experience level, context and evidence.
- Terminal: deterministic local commands with visible limits and a labeled input.
- Contact: large typographic mailto action plus GitHub and LinkedIn.
- Mobile: stacked projects, compact hero and toggleable navigation. Light mode is an optional local view.

## Constraints

Use self-hosted OFL Oxanium and original vector geometry. Do not copy commercial Cyber Grotesk files or reference artwork. Avoid dashboard/bento compositions and decorative claims of operational systems.

Keep EvalForge, OpsPilot and DocTrace offline and synthetic in their descriptions. Optional proxy-backed LLM use must remain explicit. Preserve source/tests, paid software experience and contact information. Do not add production impact or unverified availability claims.

Respect reduced motion, retain the skip link and visible focus, and keep decorative SVG/technical labels out of the accessible reading flow. Preserve one meaningful accessible heading for the wordmark.

Design tokens and implementation behavior are recorded in `../design.json` and `../../DESIGN.md`.
